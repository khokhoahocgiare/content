# Card Fusion Explorer

> **Browse cards · Discover fusions · Follow the connections**

**Card Fusion Explorer** is a visual desktop companion for exploring a large fan-made card database—not as a spreadsheet, but as a living collection of connected cards, Fusion recipes, and unexpected discoveries.

Instead of stopping at:

> *“What is this card?”*

Card Fusion Explorer encourages you to ask:

- ✨ **What can this card become?**
- 🧬 **Which cards can create it?**
- 🔗 **What other cards are connected to it?**
- 🔮 **Where will this Fusion path lead next?**

Every card can become the beginning of a new discovery.

## What is Card Fusion Explorer?

Card Fusion Explorer combines four core experiences into one polished desktop application:

- 🎴 A visual card browser
- 🔎 Advanced search and filtering
- 📖 Detailed card inspection
- 🧬 An interactive Fusion recipe explorer

Traditional card databases are excellent for looking up facts. Card Fusion Explorer focuses on what happens **after** you find a card: following its relationships, tracing Fusion paths, and uncovering combinations hidden across the collection.

```text
🎴 Card
   │
   ▼
🧬 Fusion Recipes
 ↙              ↘
🎴 Card         🎴 Card
   │               │
   ▼               ▼
More Fusions   More Fusions
   │               │
   └───────┬───────┘
           ▼
       🔮 Discover
```

## Why was it created?

Large card databases are useful, but most are built primarily for **searching**, not for **exploring**.

The usual experience often looks like this:

1. Search for a card
2. Open its details
3. Read its information
4. Go back
5. Search again
6. Repeat

For a large Fusion-focused card pool, this can quickly feel repetitive.

**Card Fusion Explorer** takes a different approach. Rather than treating each card as an isolated database record, it presents cards as connected parts of a larger system.

> **Every card should be a doorway to another discovery.**

## Fusion Explorer

The **Fusion Explorer** is the heart of Card Fusion Explorer.

A Fusion recipe can be understood like this:

```text
🎴 Card A
     +
🎴 Card B
     │
     ▼
✨ Result Card
```

But the experience does not stop at displaying one recipe.

The Fusion Explorer lets you explore relationships in both directions:

| Direction | What you can discover |
|---|---|
| ⬆️ Ingredients → Result | What can these cards create together? |
| ⬇️ Result → Ingredients | Which cards are needed to create this result? |
| 🔗 Connected paths | What other Fusions are related to these cards? |
| 🧭 Chain exploration | Where can the next Fusion path lead? |

Start from any card, open its Fusion relationships, follow an ingredient, inspect the result, then continue deeper into the collection.

A single card can reveal an entire network of possibilities.
